About Onward

Onward is a personal job application tracker for students. It tells you what to do next, drafts your follow-up messages, and celebrates your effort — not just your results.

Privacy

There's no account and no server. Your applications are stored only in this browser's localStorage, and nothing is sent anywhere. Messages are drafted for you to copy — Onward never sends emails.

Good to know

  • Your records live in this browser only and don't sync between devices.
  • Clearing your browser data removes your applications — use “Download backup” regularly.
  • Reminders appear inside Onward; there are no email or push notifications.
  • Sample companies, people and emails are fictional.
